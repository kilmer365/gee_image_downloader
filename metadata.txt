[general]
name=GEE Image Downloader
qgisMinimumVersion=3.22
qgisMaximumVersion=4.99
description=Download Landsat, Sentinel-2 and MapBiomas images straight into QGIS, including multi-year time series.
about=Pick a polygon layer, choose the imagery (Landsat 4/5/8/9, Sentinel-2 or MapBiomas) and download one date, the clearest image of a year, or a multi-year time series (for example 1995 to today), as a colour image and/or NDVI. Images are saved as 8-bit GeoTIFF files, without cloud masking and without clipping to the polygon, and opened in QGIS. Nothing goes through Google Drive. EXTERNAL DEPENDENCIES: the Python library earthengine-api (python -m pip install earthengine-api) and a Google Cloud project registered for Google Earth Engine (free for non-commercial use). The interface is available in English and Portuguese.
